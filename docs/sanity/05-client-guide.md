# Managing Finestra's products

*A guide for the Finestra team. No technical knowledge needed.*

Everything on the website's shop pages (names, prices, photos, sizes) is
managed in one place: the **Finestra Studio**.

## Getting in

1. You'll get an email invitation from Sanity. Accept it.
2. Go to **https://finestra.sanity.studio**. It works on a phone or a computer, in any browser. Bookmark it.
3. Sign in with the same Google account (or email) the invitation was sent to.

There's nothing to install and no password to share.

## What you see

On the left:

- **Products**: every product, newest first.
- **Products by category**: the same products, grouped into Rings, Bracelets, Pendants, Anime, and any categories you add.
- **Categories**: the groups themselves.

Tap a product to open it. Its details are split into three tabs: **Basics**,
**Photos**, and **Details**.

## Add a new product

1. Go to **Products by category**, choose the category, and tap **+** (the category is filled in for you). Or go to **Products** and tap **+**.
2. **Basics**
   - **Name**, e.g. Kalighat Signet.
   - **Web address**: tap **Generate**.
   - **Price (Taka)**: numbers only, e.g. 1450.
   - **Main photo**: drag a photo in, or tap to choose one. Square photos look best.
   - **Describe the photo**: one plain sentence about what's in it, e.g. "A wide silver signet ring on a black surface." This is required. Blind visitors hear it read aloud.
3. **Photos** (optional): add more angles, or the product being worn. Each needs a short description too. Drag them to change the order.
4. **Details**
   - **Material**, e.g. 925 silver.
   - **Story**: a sentence or two about the product.
   - **What the options are called**, e.g. Size, Length, Cord.
   - **Options**: type one (e.g. 18mm), press Enter, then the next.
5. Tap **Publish** (bottom right).

The product appears on the website within a few seconds, at the front of the shelf.

If **Publish** won't work, a field is missing. The Studio marks it in red and
says what's needed.

## Change a product

Open it, make the change, and tap **Publish**. Until you publish, your changes
are a **draft**: you can see them, but the website doesn't.

## Mark a product sold out

Open it, switch on **Sold out**, and tap **Publish**. It stays on the website
with a "Sold out" band across the photo. Switch it off when it's back.

## Put a product on the homepage

Switch on **On the homepage shelf** and tap **Publish**. It joins the
"On the shelf this week" row on the homepage. Switch it off to take it away.

## The order on the website

Products show newest first: the one you added most recently leads. There is
nothing to number or rearrange.

Categories do have a **Position** number, which sets the order of the filters
on the website. Lower numbers come first.

## Choose what stays in view in a photo

On a photo, tap the **crop** icon and drag the circle onto the part that must
always be visible, such as the ring's face. The website keeps that spot in
frame on every screen size.

## Hide a product without deleting it

Open it → tap the **⋯** menu next to Publish → **Unpublish**. It disappears
from the website but stays in the Studio, ready to publish again. This is the
safest choice for seasonal or paused products.

## Delete a product

Open it → **⋯** menu → **Delete** → confirm. It disappears from the website
within seconds.

- Deleted by mistake? Its **history** (the clock icon) can restore it, but **only for 3 days**. After that it's gone for good.
- If you might want it back, use **Unpublish** instead.
- Deleting a product doesn't delete its photos. They stay in the photo library and can be reused.

## Add a new category (e.g. Wallets)

Go to **Categories** → **+** → fill in **Name**, tap **Generate** for the web
address, add a **Short note**, and set a **Position** → **Publish**.

The category shows on the website as soon as at least one product in it is
published.

A category can't be deleted while products still belong to it. Move or delete
those products first.

## Good to know

- Everyone invited can add, edit, and delete everything, so share access only with people you trust.
- The website never takes orders. Customers message the shop, as before.
- Something looks wrong on the website? Tell the developer the product's name and what you changed.
