# Surface brief: Prototype 1 (Homepage + Products)

## Direction contract

THESIS: Finestra is the shelf where a man's taste in jewelry and his taste in
characters are served by the same hand. The surface refuses the category
default for men's jewelry, a dark luxury grid of floating product shots with a
thin serif and gold accents, and refuses its predictable opposite, the white
Scandinavian minimal shop. It is built instead as a hand-painted Dhaka
rickshaw panel: the visual language this audience walks past every day.

OWN-WORLD: A two-plate screenprint on white tin. Black is the ink plate,
Finestra Yellow is the colour plate, white is the tin showing through. The
rickshaw painter's polychrome is translated to two plates because the brand
guideline authorises only yellow, black, and white; the world keeps its
ornamental density, chevron border banding, floret rows, hand lettering, and
cut-out portraits, and gives up only its extra hues. Structure is a stack of
framed plates, never a row of cards. Photographs are screened toward the two
plates so they read as printed, not as stock. Bungee carries the painted
banner voice, Yellowtail the painter's script for the wordmark and tagline
only, Bricolage Grotesque the block hand for prices and running text. Every
plate is square; only chips and the primary lozenge are pill. Dark mode is the
same sign at night: black ground, yellow ink.

STORY: A visitor arriving from a Facebook or Instagram post understands within
one viewport that this is a Bangladeshi men's accessories brand with a real
identity rather than a catalogue dump, sees that classic pieces and anime
pieces sit on one shelf on purpose, and reaches a working catalogue where they
can pick a size, add to a bag, and start a checkout.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A chevron border band across the top carrying the nav and the
wordmark in script. Below it a single yellow field. Painted display lettering
sits left at two lines, the tagline curves through a painted ribbon beneath
it, and the primary lozenge sits directly under that. A cut-out photograph of
a man in a chain and bracelet stands at the right and breaks the panel frame,
overlapping the border band. Nothing is centred.

FORM: Dhaka rickshaw art and hand-painted cinema banners. Candidate 4 of seven
grounded directions. Seed key 97f1e5c1, assigned index 4, mode persuade.

RAISE (from operate-b-normalled-jackfield, declined): state is a mark, never a
hue. Selected filters, sold-out pieces, and chosen sizes are drawn with a
painted mark, so yellow stays the brand's voice instead of becoming a status
light.

RAISE (from operate-a-cutting-bench-select-rail, declined): rank by cell count
rather than by type size, and anything set aside stays visible. The bag is a
painted rail carrying the actual pieces, not a badge on an icon.

RAISE (from neubrutalist-award-grade, declined): one mechanical press rule,
tokenised once and applied to every control identically. Here it is
registration snap: elements sit with their yellow plate misregistered behind
the black, and snap into register on press.

RAISE (from exhibition-museum-curation-provenance-ribbon, declined): what is
known is separated from what is not. The catalogue is placeholder stock and
says so on the surface, rather than dressing invented prices as fact.

RAISE (from galaxies-nebulae-deep-time-accretion-disk-threshold, declined): one
governed light source. The painted highlight falls from a single direction
across every plate instead of scattering ornament evenly.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.

## Challenger verdicts

Weighed on audience identification and product clarity, fused before judging.

- neubrutalist-award-grade: declined. Holds product clarity, loses audience
  identification; it is the current tool monoculture and carries nothing of
  Dhaka or of fandom.
- posters-covers-sleeves-torn-flyer-photocopy-wall: competitive. Flyposting is
  real in this audience's street, but tear-strip navigation would wreck a
  product grid and a bag.
- galaxies-nebulae-deep-time-accretion-disk-threshold: declined on both axes.
- exhibition-museum-curation-provenance-ribbon: declined on both axes, and its
  archival buff and red ribbon conflict with the locked palette.
- operate-b-normalled-jackfield: competitive. Excellent state discipline,
  wrong world for a shop.
- operate-a-cutting-bench-select-rail: competitive. Good catalogue topology,
  weak audience identification.

No challenger won both axes, so the assigned direction builds, raised by the
five donations above.
